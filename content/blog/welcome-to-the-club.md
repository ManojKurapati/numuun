Let’s get one thing straight: you are not “just” a parent. You are the
lead engineer on the most advanced project on the planet — a human brain
that is wiring itself faster than at any other point in life.

Every time you narrate what you’re cooking, sing the same song for the
hundredth time, or play peek-a-boo until your face hurts, you’re laying down
the foundations for how your kid will talk, think, move and love.

> That’s not a chore list. That’s a superpower.

## What you’ll find here

- **Milestones, decoded.** What most kids are doing at each age, from
  The Smiler at two months to The Big Kid at five.
- **Play ideas that actually work.** Five-minute, zero-prep, no-fancy-toys
  stuff you can do between naps.
- **Real talk.** No guilt trips, no comparison traps. Every kid has their own
  route — we’re just here with the map.

## One promise

We’ll celebrate the wins with you — the first giggle, the first wobbly step,
the fortieth “but *why*?”. Because they’re a big deal. And so are you.
